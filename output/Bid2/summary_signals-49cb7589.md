# Bid summary
The State of Maryland Treasurer's Office issued a Request for Proposal (RFP) (solicitation BPM044557) for Dell Latitude 5550 laptops with extended warranties and related accessories, including the Dell Thunderbolt 4 Dock. The laptops must be Microsoft Copilot ready and meet detailed specifications covering processor, memory, storage, display, and networking features. The procurement is conducted under a master contract with a Small Business Reserve designation, requiring bids to be submitted through the state e-procurement system by a specified deadline. Submission rules, evaluation criteria, and contact information are detailed in the Purchase Order Request for Proposals (PORFP). Vendors must provide several affidavits, including a Contract Affidavit affirming legal compliance with Maryland procurement laws and a Mercury Affidavit declaring the presence or absence of mercury in offered products. The Contract Affidavit also covers business registration, financial and political contribution disclosures for contracts over $100,000 and $200,000 respectively, and certification of a drug and alcohol-free workplace. All affidavits require authorized signatures affirming accuracy under penalty of perjury.
