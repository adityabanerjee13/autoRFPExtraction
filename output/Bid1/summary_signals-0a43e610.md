# Bid summary
The Dallas Independent School District issued RFP JA-207652 for procuring student and staff computing devices, outlining detailed technical specifications, pricing, and contract terms in compliance with federal regulations (EDGAR 2 CFR Part 200). The solicitation includes requirements for vendor questionnaires, legal and insurance agreements, background checks, conflict of interest disclosures, and Form 1295 submission. Evaluation criteria emphasize best value, M/WBE compliance, and adherence to procurement policies, including diversity participation goals and integration with Dallas ISD’s procurement system. Addendum No. 1 clarifies device specifications, warranty terms, delivery schedules, and submission requirements, requiring vendor acknowledgment by signature. Addendum No. 2 extends the proposal submission deadline without altering other terms and also requires signed acknowledgment from bidders. The bid information, hosted on BidNet Direct, provides key dates, contact details, and general RFP scope for interested vendors.
